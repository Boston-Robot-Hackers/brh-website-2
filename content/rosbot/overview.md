title: Build-A-ROSBot
---

**Build-A-ROSBot** is a Boston Robot Hackers program where participants build a small ROS-based robot over a series of hands-on sessions.

**The current session is fully subscribed**

In this program, participants will work individually and together on assembling a robot using the Maker's Pet kit. It is a relatively easy kit to assemble, if you are somewhat handy with tools. 

The kit comes With all necessary parts, electronics, screws and bolts, wheels, and miscellaneous other items. Based on our careful search, it is the least expensive robot which we were able to find that runs ROS2. We will have face-to-face sessions as well in order to help people get that aspect complete.

We will also learn the basics of ROS, the Robot Operating System. This learning will be based on specific mini-projects which will create interesting behaviors in the robot in the physical world while allowing participants to better understand the underlying robotic fundamentals.

We are still evolving the schedule and program. There will be Zoom meetings as well as in-person meetings. Their pace will be based on the progress made by the participants. It will require a substantial amount of time from you, but the outcome will be very rewarding and exciting.

## Participation

This program is based entirely on ROS 2 and Maker's Pet. Because of that, we will give preference to people who have physical Maker's Pets. We will not accept people who just want to watch or observe; everyone should have a robot of some kind. Those who are not working on a Maker's Pet are considered to be auditing the program.
