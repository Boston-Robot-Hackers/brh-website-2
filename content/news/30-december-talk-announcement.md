### Featured Talk

#### Robotics in the Real World

Addisu Taddese, the lead maintainer of Gazebo, will give us insights from the inside. What is it like to work on a robotics simulation platform that is used by thousands of people around the world? What are the challenges and opportunities in robotics simulation?

#### Speaker: Addisu Taddese

Addisu Taddese is passionate about applying robotics research and advanced algorithms to hard problems. He brings both academic research experience in robotics and hands-on embedded hardware and software development from industry. He has a strong record of academic achievement, leadership, and teamwork, and is active in community outreach through robotics education and open source development.
