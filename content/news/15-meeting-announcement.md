**COMING SOON**

**Pito Salas* On January 8, 2026, will be giving a talk on URDF and Vibe Coding. The talk will cover URDF (the unvirsal robot description format), teaching you the basics, discuss the complexity and user-un-friendlyness of URDF, then talk about usign "AI" (Claude.ai) to greatly simplify and automate working with it. I will also talk about a couple of approaches I've investigated to work with URDF at a higher level of abstraction.

*Chris Lai* On March 5, 2026, will be giving a talk entitled: "Teaching Advanced Robotic Concepts to High School Students". He will introduce or re-introduce us to Kalman filters and how to think about them. With code examples!

*Alan Kilian* On April 2, 2026, will be giving our main talk on "Electrical Design of a robot", covering power supply, battery types, charging batteries, amps, watts, volts, mah, etc. What components are typical/popular in different scenarios.

*Alan Kilian* On June 11, 2026, will be giving our main talk on "Motors for robots: Types of motors, how to pick the right one for your application, motor controller boards"
