### Lightning Talk

To help spread our expertise around and make it easy and fun to learn from each other we are now going to have a short section of "lightning talks" each meeting. A lightning talk is a really quick 5-10 minute talk presented by one of us. Topics are anything robot-related where you explain something you know, or know how to do, or show, so we all learn from each other.

IIf you have any cool tool, technique, idea, or challenge that would be interesting for the rest of us, we’d love to get you to do a lightning talk. We have a variety of levels of members and like to have these talks aimed at any of them. Some people are professionals with years of ROS2 experience. Some are motivated beginners and some are just coming up to speed. Share your knowledge. We all discover a cool tool here and there!

Email Pito if you have an idea that you want to share!
