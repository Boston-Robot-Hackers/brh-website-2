### Featured Talk

#### The Unlikely Story of Roomba: Birthing the World's Favorite Robot

This presentation recounts an improbable journey through decades of failed robot-vacuum attempts to the creation and success of Roomba. Starting in the 1950s, evidence of market interest and technical feasibility sparked efforts by major corporations and basement inventors alike to design a robot floor cleaner. But practical constraints thwarted all contenders. A breakthrough at the MIT Artificial Intelligence Lab in the 1980s, a shift in perspective, plus abundant perseverance changed the equation.

Drawing on the speaker's robotics career, the presentation follows the path from robotics research and early floor-sweeping prototypes to the invention of the original Roomba at iRobot. Real world challenges, engineering choices, and perplexing setbacks on the road to Roomba's 2002 launch are outlined. The reworking of a scrappy startup into an established company is also sketched. This talk will interest anyone wishing to know the true story of how an appealing notion transformed from science fiction to a practical product welcomed into millions of homes.

#### Speaker: Joe Jones

Joe Jones is a seasoned robotics innovator with over four decades of experience. After earning two degrees from MIT, Joe spent nine years on the research staff at the MIT Artificial Intelligence Lab (now CSAIL) where, in 1989, he built an early prototype of a floor-cleaning robot.

Joe then joined fledgling iRobot where he proposed the iconic Roomba robot vacuum. He then served as Roomba's chief architect. Joe went on to co-found Harvest Automation, functioning as CTO and helping to create a robot for the nursery and greenhouse industry. More recently, Joe founded Tertill Corporation, where he developed Tertill, a robot that weeds home gardens.

Before his most recent work of narrative nonfiction, *Dancing with Roomba* (CRC Press, 2025), Joe authored three earlier technical books on robotics. He holds over 80 US and several foreign patents.
